We are a community of communities, built on and furthering openness, trust, and quality. Our members seek to promote and defend the rights of open source projects and create a better web.

Affiliates

The Charter allows any organization to be given affiliate status. Their representatives can attend meetings, but do not have voting rights.

  • FAIR Package Manager

Contact

Media and membership enquiries can be directed to the president of the Open Website Alliance. The current president is Mary Hubbard from WordPress Foundation.

Prospective members should read our membership application process before making contact.

For support questions regarding your content management system, please refer to the respective software project’s support pages.

    Ahead of CloudFest, TYPO3 outlined plans to work with other open-source contributors on FAIR protocol support, connecting the proposal to its work within the Alliance.
